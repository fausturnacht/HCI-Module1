Manila Half-Day Sightseeing Tour

Overview ▼

Discover the rich history and vibrant culture of Manila on this half-day sightseeing tour. Visit the historic walled city of Intramuros, explore Fort Santiago, and see modern landmarks like Rizal Park and the Manila Cathedral. This tour offers a perfect blend of old and new, providing an in-depth look at the Philippines' capital.

Highlights ▼

  • Explore the historic walled city of Intramuros
  • Visit Fort Santiago, a 16th-century military fortress
  • See Rizal Park, a tribute to the national hero Jose Rizal
  • Admire the architecture of Manila Cathedral
  • Learn about Manila's colonial past and modern development

Itinerary

Choose from a morning or afternoon tour that starts with pickup from your Manila hotel, then travel with your experienced guide into the heart of the Philippine's financial center: Makati.

    Rizal Park, also known as Luneta Park or simply Luneta, is a historic urban park located in Ermita, Manila, Philippines which is considered as one of the largest urban parks in Asia. Here you will see the statue of national hero Jose Rizal and other historical monuments.

    10 minutes • Admission Ticket Free
  2. 2

    San Agustin Church - Immaculate Conception Parish

    San Agustin Church, also known as the Archdiocesan Shrine of Nuestra Señora de Consolación y Correa or the Immaculate Conception Parish, is a Roman Catholic church under the auspices of the Order of St. Augustine, located in the historic walled city of Intramuros in Manila, Philippines.

    15 minutes • Admission Ticket Included
  3. 3

    Manila American Cemetery and Memorial

    The Manila American Cemetery and Memorial is located in Fort Bonifacio, Taguig City, Metro Manila, within the boundaries of the former Fort William McKinley. It can be reached most easily from the city via Epifanio de los Santos Avenue (EDSA).

    15 minutes • Admission Ticket Free
  4. 4

    Intramuros

    Old-world Intramuros is home to Spanish-era landmarks like Fort Santiago, with a large stone gate and a shrine to national hero Jose Rizal.

    30 minutes • Admission Ticket Included
  5. 5

    Roxas Boulevard (Pass By)

    Roxas Boulevard is a popular waterfront promenade in Metro Manila in the Philippines. The boulevard, which runs along the shores of Manila Bay, is well known for its sunsets and stretch of coconut trees.

    Pass By
  6. 6

    Fort Santiago

    Fort Santiago, built in 1593, is a citadel built by Spanish navigator and governor Miguel López de Legazpi for the new established city of Manila in the Philippines. The defense fortress is located in Intramuros, the historic walled area of Manila.

    20 minutes • Admission Ticket Included
